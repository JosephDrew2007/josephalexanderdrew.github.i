Why marketing and law

Two sides of the same skill.

Marketing and law both come down to persuasion built on trust. Marketing teaches me how people think and what moves them. Law teaches me precision, ethics, and what a message is allowed to promise.

Together they make me useful to any team where the message and the rules both matter.

Where I'm headed: marketing and media roles now, then law school, with a focus on areas where business and law overlap, such as media, entertainment, and brand protection.
