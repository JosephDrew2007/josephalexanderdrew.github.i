About

Where business meets law, and discipline meets daily practice.

I'm Joseph Alexander Drew, a sophomore in the UConn School of Business studying marketing, from Fairfield, Connecticut. I plan to attend law school after graduation.

I work as a legal assistant intern at a law office in Westport, chair the BLAW committee in UConn's Business Connections Learning Community, and manage social media for UConn Project Cinema. Outside class I journal every day and train in Muay Thai, wrestling, and lifting.

In high school I served three years as class vice president, earned my Eagle Scout rank, and placed 3rd in the state in DECA before competing at Internationals in Anaheim.
